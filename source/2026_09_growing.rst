Growing (September 2026)
========================

Shown here is a grow inside a grow box measuring 2' deep, 3' tall and 4' wide. There are a couple
of 80mm fans, one pushing and one pulling. There are 8x 4' 6500k fluorescent bulbs in a custom
built fixture. Soil moisture is monitored via the weight of the grow over time.

In the plots, you will see "Tare A,B,C".

Tare A is the water-back set point. This is the weight to which the grow is watered back to with
each irrigation event. It helps me as the gardener maintain a desired moisture level. Notice that
the target soil moisture is fine tuned through the time period shown. As the gardener, I can
adjust the set point as I perceive the plants' needs.

Tare B is the absolute weight of the grow.

I use Tare C for various things, such as measuring harvest.
